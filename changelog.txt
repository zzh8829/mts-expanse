Version: 0.1.18
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - A hidden mission hub full of asteroid chunks no longer blocks newly unlocked rewards.
      Each reward item can use a small, bounded overflow inventory when the shared hub is full.
    - Cargo delivery resumes after the last stack sent so a busy hatch cannot continually
      prioritize early asteroid stacks over later rewards.
    - Overflow inventories preserve item quality and spoilage and are cleaned up on team reset
      or release. Existing rewards, landing pad requests, and capacity checks are preserved.
  Tests:
    - Passed 102 native cargo checks across standalone and MTS games with both support modes,
      including full-hub starvation, slow hatches, bounded storage, and actual pod arrival.
    - Verified 71 mission progression steps and invasion triggering after paid cell unlocks
      and save/reload. This release does not change invasion rules.

Version: 0.1.17
Date: 25. 09. 2026
  Features:
    - Space Age mission reward drops now respect the receiving landing pad's requests, including
      item quality, section multipliers, disabled requests, and circuit-controlled requests.
      Requests are stock targets: pad contents and incoming cargo count toward the requested amount.
  Bugfixes:
    - Scripted reward pods reserve available landing pad space before launch, including shared
      slots and incoming cargo. Full or missing pads retain goods at the mission source for retry.
    - Pads without configured requests keep automatic reward delivery, limited by free space.
    - One-time mission rewards that do not fit at the source are saved and retried without loss.
      Passive production rates are unchanged; production pauses when source storage is full.
  Tests:
    - Added native Factorio request, capacity, circuit, quality, and actual pod arrival regressions
      for standalone and MTS games with NonOrbit and orbit-platform support.

Version: 0.1.16
Date: 22. 09. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed recycled team slots spawning on NonOrbit or another team's surface after a mod update.
      Gameplay surfaces are now checked by name as well as index, and released team state is cleared.
    - Mod updates no longer recreate mission support surfaces for retired teams. Affected occupied
      teams recover their existing Expanse world, or a new starting cell if their old world was deleted.
      Healthy factories, research, and mission progress are preserved.
  Tests:
    - Added native Factorio regressions for reused surface indices, upgrade recovery, team release,
      and preserving another team's world in vanilla and Space Age.

Date: 19. 09. 2026
  Changes:
    - Reduced the default time evolution factor from 0.000002 to 0.0000005: time alone

Version: 0.1.6
Date: 29. 05. 2026
  Features:
    - Per-team milestones via Multi-Team Support: first/fastest to unlock Expanse cells
      (1st, 10th ... 10000th) and to mine the infini tree / rock (100 ... 100000 times),
      shown in the Team Awards window.
    - An on-surface overlay above each team's spawn cell shows the team (colour + leader),
      cells unlocked, and the next biter invasion -- readable by the team and by spectators.
    - Mined wood and the rock's ore and stone are registered in production statistics, so
      they show per team in the native Production GUI (and the MTS Ores awards/columns).
  Changes:
    - Hungry chests are now fully independent per team: rerolling or feeding one team's
      chest never changes another team's chest that they have not opened yet.
    - Cross-team parity: tree-regrowth species and rock yields (ore type + amount) are
      deterministic by shared seed, position, and mine index, so every team's Nth tree and
      Nth rock match. Bonus reroll tokens and depleted-stone uranium are position-keyed too.
    - Destroying the rock with a tank (or any team weapon) now counts toward the mining
      milestone, like hand or robot mining; biter kills do not.
    - MTS integration (welcome tab, team-settings tab, milestones) self-heals on load.
  Info:
    - Requires Multi-Team Support 0.4.44+ for the milestone interface.

Version: 0.1.4
Date: 28. 05. 2026
  Features:
    - Under Multi-Team Support, the map intro now shows as a tab on MTS's unified
      welcome screen, and the Expanse player settings (SpectatorMode, button position)
      show as a tab on MTS's Team Settings panel, instead of the standalone Config GUI.
      Requires Multi-Team Support 0.4.43+ for the welcome-screen integration.
  Changes:
    - No spawn flash: vanilla teams now play directly on the surface MTS spawns them
      onto. The Expanse world is generated on Nauvis and replicated to each team by
      MTS's clone_mirror, so players are never relocated to a throwaway surface.
    - Team setup is driven by MTS's on_player_joined_team event, and surfaces are
      resolved via MTS's get_surface_owner where available.
    - The standalone Config GUI (and its admin Blueprint Library / Spaghett options) is
      hidden under MTS but unchanged in non-MTS games.
  Bugfixes:
    - Cross-team parity: per-cell randomness (including the starting tree species) is
      now keyed by planet and position, so every team gets identical content.
    - Team hosts are no longer auto-promoted to Factorio admin (that granted server
      admin to anyone who started a team); the /expanse-* commands still require admin.
    - A team leader spectating another team is no longer pulled back to their base.
  Optimizations:
    - The hungry-chest scan is skipped for teams with nobody online.
