Version: 0.1.25
Date: 19. 11. 2026
  Major Features:
    - Runs on Factorio 2.1. Multi-Team Support 0.7.0 or newer is a recommended dependency: the
      game switches it on by default and it can be switched off for standalone play.
      0.1.24 is the last version for Factorio 2.0. Saves from 2.0 load and keep their missions,
      silos, cargo chests and research.
  Features:
    - Hover tooltips. A mission silo says its mission and step, what is still to launch and
      the reward of the step. A cargo chest says which rocket it feeds. A hungry chest lists
      what it still wants by name. The landing pad lists what arrives and how often.
    - Map pins. "Pin silo on my screen" on the mission board pins that silo for the player
      who clicks. When a silo is uncovered every team member gets a pin with the chat line.
    - Team alerts while something is stuck: rewards waiting because the landing pad is full,
      a mission silo with cargo waiting and no power, and a cargo chest that robots hold
      items for and cannot reach.
    - The silo side panel shows two numbers per item, still to launch and already in the
      cargo chest, and the cargo chest's own slots. Items can be moved in and out there,
      shift-click and control-click included.
    - New startup setting "Mission rocket cargo": 1 t (default), 2 t, 3 t, 4 t or 5 t per
      mission rocket. The launch weight, the silo label, the panel, the mission board and
      the cargo chest's requests follow it.
  Changes:
    - Space Age without the Quality mod, which Factorio 2.1 allows: a mission step that asks
      for a higher quality asks for more normal items instead, 2 for uncommon, 4 for rare,
      8 for epic and 16 for legendary. The orbit platform is built from normal parts.
    - The orbit platform of the platform mode is created without a chat announcement.
    - The "Rocket launch weight threshold" setting is given for a 1 t rocket and scales with
      the mission rocket cargo setting.
    - The spectator switch in the config window is gone. Factorio 2.1 removed the player
      property behind it, which never did anything.
  Bugfixes:
    - Loading a 2.0 save on 2.1 switched the rocket silo recipe back on until the team's next
      research. The recipe ban is applied on a game version change as well.
    - A reset, forfeit or surface cleanup moves the player's body when the player is looking
      at the map. Since 2.1 a teleport in map view moves only the camera.
    - Mission silo repair keeps cargo, modules and trash on 2.1, where a rocket's inventory
      has no fixed slots and the silo's crafting inventories have new names.
    - Silo labels drawn by an older version are rewritten after the update.
  Tests:
    - All suites pass on Factorio 2.1.25 with Multi-Team Support 0.7.15. The save upgrade
      tests make their old save on Factorio 2.0.77 and load it on 2.1.
    - New scripts/test-windows.py: 34 checks that open and operate the mission board, the
      silo side panel and the reset dialog with a real player, in the game's test mode.
    - New scripts/test-rocket-lift.py: the mission rocket cargo setting at 1 t and 3 t.

Version: 0.1.24
Date: 19. 11. 2026
  Major Features:
    - The Space missions window is a mission board. The seven ships are listed by name with
      their state in words. The selected mission shows every item by name with its progress,
      what is delivered and what is still to launch, the reward of the step written out, and
      how to deliver. "What you receive" lists what the ships send down. Escape and E close it.
    - Every mission silo has a cargo chest and a constant combinator beside it, placed by the
      mod. The chest requests what the current step still needs, less what is already in the
      chest, in the rocket or in flight. Robots, inserters and players can fill it. A ready
      rocket is loaded from the chest and launched for you.
    - A part load launches by itself when nothing more that the step needs fits, or when the
      last items of the step are aboard. The combinator outputs what still has to be brought.
  Features:
    - The mission silo window has a side panel: the step, what is still to load, the cargo
      weight, the launch rule in one sentence, and a Launch now button.
    - Mission silos show their ship name and status above them without Alt mode. Found silos
      and the landing pad have map tags.
    - Team chat has one line when a mission opens, when a silo or the landing pad is uncovered,
      and when a step is done, with the reward and the rockets the next step needs. Only the
      owning team sees them. The Discord line names the team.
    - Resetting a base is one window, "Reset your base?". It lists what is removed and what is
      kept, and the red button works after RESET is typed.
    - The welcome text is rewritten and has a space missions paragraph in Space Age.
    - Hungry chests show the remaining count under each icon without Alt mode. A re-roll is
      announced to the chest's own team only.
  Changes:
    - Gaia steps 4, 6, 8 and 10 pay 2 more yumako and jellynut per minute. They used to add to
      the spoil time of the whole game, which every team shared. Teams already past those
      steps receive the fruit once on update.
    - /expanse-forfeit and /expanse-reset from the console act on an existing team only, and
      forfeit needs the confirm word there too.
  Bugfixes:
    - Multi-Team Support event ids are looked up every session. A stored id went stale when
      another mod changed its event count: joins were refused with "mod event handlers are not
      identical", or a handler ran on the wrong event.
    - Missions whose technology is already researched open again after a reset and on update.
      A reset used to lock a team out of space missions for good.
    - Repairing the Galactica silo raised an error.
    - Biome decals are converted on the planet cells. The conversion never ran before, and two
      Aquilo names were misspelled.
    - The welcome window keeps its own width. Its paragraphs stretched it across the screen.
    - The mission board scrolls a long step inside the window. A step with more than twenty
      items made the window taller than the screen.
  Tests:
    - New scripts/test-mission-station.py: 14 native checks of the cargo station, a part-load
      launch that completes a step, reset unlock, command targets under MTS, decal conversion
      and the Gaia migration, standalone and with MTS.
